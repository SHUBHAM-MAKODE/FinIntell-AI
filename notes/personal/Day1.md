Details about the project in Simple terms

FinInetll AI is a smart personal finance maagement application that helps people track their expenses, manage budgets, and understand where their money goes using AI.

Think of it as a combination of an expense tracker, a budget planner, and a personal financial assistant.

Example: How it works

Suppose you earn ₹30,000 per month.

With FinIntell AI, you can:

→ Track expenses: Record expenses such as ₹5,000 for groceries, ₹2,000 for travel, and ₹3,000 for shopping.

→ Manage budgets: Set a monthly budget of ₹10,000 for groceries and track how much you've spent.

→ Upload receipts: Upload a bill, and AI automatically extracts the amount, vendor, and date so you don't have to enter everything manually.

→ Ask questions: Ask the AI, "How much money did I spend this month?" or "Which category costs me the most?"

→ View your finances: See your spending patterns, remaining budgets, and expense breakdowns on a dashboard.


Break down of work 

User → Enters expenses or uploads receipts → Application processes and saves the data → AI analyzes relevant financial information → User sees results on the dashboard or receives answers through chat.

What problem it solves?
Many people don't know:

1. Where their money goes every month.
2. Which expenses consume most of their income.
3. Whether they are exceeding their budgets.
4. How their spending habits change over time.

FinIntell AI helps them answer these questions without manually analyzing spreadsheets or calculating everything themselves.
